## 0. Summary

1. **Front side under the display: parts up to 1.0 mm; the body grows to 25 mm** ([ADR 0016](../adr/0016-front-side-height-under-the-display.md)). The thickness stack gave 0.3 mm between the display and the board, while about 2,100–3,700 mm² of parts must sit under the display. The extra 1.0 mm comes from body thickness (T1-031 maximum), not from the battery, whose energy is the limit of T1-039. No thickness margin is left within 25 mm.
2. **High-speed values come from Rockchip's documents** (Rockchip HDG 1.1 and HSPCB 1.0: RK3566 Hardware Design Guide V1.1.0 and the RK3568 High Speed PCB Design Guide V1.0.0, which covers the same interface blocks). LPDDR4X is 50 Ω single-ended and 100 Ω differential. Where Rockchip is looser than a standard or than the T1's need, the T1 keeps the tighter value and says why.
3. **The main board is an HDI build, without via-in-pad as a requirement.** The RK3566 has 343 core balls at 0.65 mm and a two-row ring of 222 balls at 0.4 mm. Laser microvias as dog-bones in the 0.825–0.875 mm channel between the ring and the core escape every inner-ring ball (arithmetic on the package drawing, Rockchip HDG 1.1 and HSPCB 1.0); through vias do not fit there. Rockchip itself uses 4–6 layers; the T1's six ([stackup v3](stackup-v3.md)) are the minimum that meets every must constraint (PCB-S-03), and the escape study closes the escape, the decoupling and the core planes on them.
4. **Copper for power is sized for the protection rating, not the typical draw** (BRD-049): the battery path carries the 5 A fuse rating continuously with ≤ 10 °C rise.
5. **Audio rules come from the CS43198 and OPA1622 datasheets** and from the decided topology (ADR 0010, 0012, 0015): split nothing, partition everything.
6. **The battery connects through six spring contacts in a window of the pocket floor** ([ADR 0020](../adr/0020-battery-connected-through-contacts.md); v0.9): two contacts per power pole, an empty position between P+ and the signals, only battery nets and GND in the contact area, and the ESD diodes at the contacts (PCB-L-04 to L-10). DRC checks the partition, the empty position and the ESD placement; heights, tolerances and pour symmetry are CHK and REV items.
7. **Withdrawn ([RFC 0014](https://github.com/opendap-project/rfcs/blob/main/text/0014-hat-interface-v2-usb-only.md), v0.12).** HATs are USB only (hat-standard [interface v2](https://github.com/opendap-project/hat-standard/blob/main/docs/specs/interface-v2.md)): the HAT extension, its switches and its PCIe and CSI-2 rows are gone. The HAT port is USB 3 (`USB3_90D`), its USB 2.0 pair (`USB2_90D`), `CC` (PCB-I-14) and `VBUS` with its ground return at the interface's 4.5 A fault ceiling (PCB-P-20, P-21).
8. **The board is six layers, HDI 2+2+2, 1.046 mm nominal, zoned** ([stackup v3](stackup-v3.md), [floorplan v1](floorplan-v1.md); v0.15; was eight layers in stackup v2 and ten in stackup v1). Six zones share the layers, each with its own roles; one full-layer ground (L5) and zone-local grounds on L2 and L3 stitched to it, one ground net (PCB-A-01). Impedance classes route on L1, L4 and L6 where the zone's references are ground (PCB-S-14), and each class has a width per layer (PCB-I). The maintainers accepted nine trades with the stack (stackup v3 §0.1), each a row here (PCB-S-20 to S-25, PCB-P-22, P-23, PCB-A-20). The stack rests on the fab's published data and stated assumptions until the fab's DFM review, so its width rules are warnings until then.

## 9. Open items

1. **Front-side height (PCB-Z-03):** decided by [ADR 0016](../adr/0016-front-side-height-under-the-display.md). Still open from its study: package heights of the RK3566, LPDDR4X and eMMC; the back bands at 104–123 % of their area with today's tall parts (4020 inductors, haptic motor, microSD socket); the microSD socket fits neither the top band nor the zone under the battery. Both go to placement.
2. **Rockchip documents** retrieved (part 1). Still missing: the RK3566 edition of the high-speed guide, RK3566 DDR templates, package pin lengths, a land pattern (Rockchip publishes none: the footprint is drawn from the package drawing and checked by a second reading) and IBIS models. LPDDR4X needs 1.10 V (VDD2) besides 0.60 V (VDDQ) at the SoC: check against the power tree.
3. **Murata Type 2EL:** application note, footprint and certification note retrieved (the USB, PCIe and MIPI D-PHY specifications); the datasheet is still missing. Findings for the power tree and the product: the module needs a 1.8 V power rail (AVDD18, 323–390 mA typical in Bluetooth/802.15.4 transmit, peaks above 1 A on the carrier datasheet) besides 3.3 V; the US and Canada modular approvals cover mobile use at ≥ 20 cm only, so the handheld T1 needs SAR and a permissive change through Murata; the Molex 146187 antenna is on Japan's list only (with a ≥ 50 mm cable).
4. **Standards** (the USB, PCIe and MIPI D-PHY specifications): USB 2.0 and USB 3.2 texts retrieved (USB 2.0 allows ±15 %, the board keeps the fab's ±10 %; USB 3 AC caps 75–265 nF); PCIe and MIPI from vendor guides only (member-only specs); the eMMC 5.1 text and SD SDR104 timing are still missing.
5. **Case inputs**: walls, bosses, clearances (PCB-M-02, PCB-M-08) through the t1 interface files.
6. **The fab**: HDI stackup, microvia land, shielding frames, fiducial practice.
7. **DFM values** ([`hardware/rules/README.md`](../../hardware/rules/README.md#review-and-script-items-chk)): the screw, washer and hole (PCB-H-01 to H-06), copper and parts at break tabs and edges (PCB-B-01, B-02), BGA rework clearance (PCB-D-01, T-12), the open-via distance (PCB-D-10), silk to edge (PCB-D-05), the battery pocket margin (PCB-L-01). Its CHK and REV items carry what DRC cannot check (thermal spokes and uncoupled length wait for kicad-lib's `check_drc.py` to read KiCad's unnamed messages).
8. **Industry-wisdom values:** stitching fence pitch (PCB-S-13), rail-to-input separation (PCB-A-15), shared-return resistance (PCB-A-17), headphone RF filter values (PCB-A-19), TPS65131 power-save pin and the AUDIO_N7V0 minimum load (PCB-P-13), mating-order clamping (PCB-P-17), antenna distances (PCB-R-06, R-07, R-13) and the case-antenna matching values (PCB-R-10), the ESD region (PCB-E-05) and TVS via count (PCB-E-04), probe pad size (PCB-T-10), the touch-temperature target (PCB-L-03).
