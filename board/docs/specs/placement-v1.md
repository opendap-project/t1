## 4. Decisions

Each changes floorplan v1 where the real footprints did not fit it; the tool's `plan.py` carries the same rows.

1. **Accepted (2026-10-27): the audio zone grows to y 2.4 on every layer and to y 14 on the front (x < −6.2).** Basis: its parts need 1,665 mm² of courtyard against 1,557 mm² of floorplan zone on both sides (§3.1); the core zone has room (§3.2); above y 2.4 the eMMC and its rework clearance (PCB-D-01) keep the back and inner layers; a zone may cover only some layers where two zones share a spot (floorplan v1 §2). The core–audio corridor moves to x −11 to −5, y −1 to 5, across the new boundary.
2. **Accepted (2026-10-27): the power zone grows to y 0 (x > 6.2), its left edge moves to x 4.0 between the battery-contact window and y 0, it takes the front of the HAT strip right of the faceplate area, and the back of x −2 to 4, y −18.8 to −8.** Basis: the power zone's parts over 1.0 mm stand on the back only (twelve 0805 capacitors of the charger, five INA236, the ship FET, diodes), and its back outside the contact window is full of them; the I/O zone uses 30 % of its front (§3.1); the I/O pairs pass the moved back area on L4 and L1. The core–power corridor moves to x 8 to 14, y −3 to 3.
3. **Accepted (2026-10-27): parts over 1.5 mm stand in the bands at fixed places.** The power band above the USB-C (taken from the I/O zone's back) holds the battery fuse under the P+ contacts; the right band the VSYS_LV and pre-rail inductors (XGL4020, 2.1 mm) and, since v1.3, the pre-rail's three 1206 output capacitors around its inductor's output pad (decision 14). Until v1.2 the band above the USB-C also held the 5.1 V output 1210 (two 0805 since BOM v1.18 item 5, placed with their converter) and one pre-rail 1206. Decision 12 took the vdd_cpu, vcc3v3_sys, radio AVDD18, charger and 5.1 V inductors out of the bands; the core top band (x −5.5 to 5.6, taken from the radio zone left of the microSD) stays with the core. Basis: PCB-Z-01, Z-02; the converters stand opposite their inductors on the front. Cost (until decision 12): the vdd_cpu converter sat about 24 mm from the SoC's package edge.
4. **Withdrawn (2026-10-27, decision 13): no part over 1.0 mm stands on the front's bottom strip.** In the 55.3 × 90.1 mm outline the VSYS_LV converter's 22 µF 0805 C904 (1.25 mm) found no back room and stood there on PCB-Z-04's 6.8 mm (**UNVERIFIED**); in the wider power zone the tool places it on the back next to C903, 3.6 mm from its converter.
5. **Accepted (2026-10-27): the RK809-5 stands above the SoC, at (−4.8, 24.6) on the back** (v1.3; was (−6.0, 25.7), where its top row of pins stood 0.2 mm from the radio zone and the last of its 27 capacitors found no room within its bound, decision 14). Basis: below the SoC the grown audio and power zones leave about 10 × 8 mm, and the PMIC with its five 1.2 mm inductors (back only) needs about 150 mm²; above, it sits next to the vdd_cpu buck, 2 mm from the SoC's and the eMMC's courtyards (PCB-D-01). Floorplan v1 had it below the SoC.
6. **Accepted (2026-10-27): the RP2354A stands at (−16.0, 24.5) on the front**, clear of the eMMC. Basis: its exposed pad's thermal vias go through the board, so the other side under them must be free of pads (PCB-V-01, shorts); the audio zone took its floorplan place.
7. **Accepted (2026-10-27): `blk.audio-supply` (TPS62840 buck, its inductor and switches) belongs to the power zone.** Basis: switch nodes stay in the power zone except the PMIC's and the vdd_cpu buck's (floorplan v1 §4 item 6, PCB-A-06); floorplan v1 §2 listed the block under `blk.audio`.

9. **Accepted (2026-10-27; revised 2026-10-28): flat test pads stand in the zones of their nets, on either side.** Basis: [ADR 0027](../adr/0027-flying-probe-test-for-prototypes-and-first-runs.md) (flying probe for prototypes and first runs, reaching both sides; until 2026-10-28 the front only, for ADR 0016's single-sided fixture); PCB-S-17 (a test pad's track stays in its net's zone). Basis: the study's matrices: same inductance, design peak ≤ 70 % and maximum current limit ≤ 100 % of Isat at 30 % drop (BRD-049 as power tree v0 §4.10 applies it), heights PCB-Z-01 and Z-03, ratings at least twice the rail, PCB-D-06; sourcing check (T1-029), 2026-10-27: open for XGL4015-102MEC and conditional for XGL4015-471MEC (BOM v1.15), with the 2.1 mm parts on the same land as the fallback. 13. **Accepted (2026-10-27): the board takes the 62.5 × 95.0 mm body** ([ADR 0025](../adr/0025-height-zones-in-the-wider-body.md), [t1 ADR 0006](../../../product/docs/adr/0006-body-at-the-maximum-width.md)). `pcb/geometry.py` derives the outline from the body, the 1.2 mm walls and the 0.5 mm clearance (59.1 × 91.6 mm), keeps the pocket, the display and every fixed part at their device-frame positions (the bands become 17.25 and 7.25 mm from the board edges), keeps the holes' distance to the edges, and turns the speaker's band shadow into a case shelf over parts up to 1.5 mm (PCB-Z-09). Parts placed against an edge follow it (jacks, USB-C, microSD, side buttons, cover-detect pads). The 0201 swaps of BOM v1.16 apply. Basis: the area result of ADR 0025 (111 % → 98.8 % by §3.1's method; factor left 1.49 → 1.68).
14. **Accepted (2026-10-27): every converter's input and output capacitors and every IC's decoupling stand within a bound of the pins they serve, and the build fails otherwise** (`pcb/proximity.py`). Which capacitor serves which pins comes from the netlist's pin functions and types and from the generator: a converter (a part with a pin on a switch node) has input capacitors (between a supply-input net of it and its ground; an inverting converter's ground is its negative rail) and output capacitors (between the far net of its inductor and its ground, served at the inductor's output pad); any other capacitor to ground on a supply pin or regulator output of an IC of its block decouples it; a capacitor the generator marks in its `Decoupling` field (the decoupling banks, such as the SoC's) serves its rail's supply pins on the board. Distance: the capacitor's pad on the served net to the nearest served pad, centre to centre, either side. Bounds: input 3.0 mm (the hot loop, PCB-P-07), output 5.0 mm, decoupling 4.0 mm, 10 µF and more 6.0 mm (PCB-P-10). Basis: the data sheets ask for these capacitors as close as possible and show them adjacent in their layout examples without a number (SLVSDV2 §12.1–12.2, SLVSES3A §10.1–10.2, SNVSAN3F §10.1–10.2, SBVS338H §7.4); PCB-P-07 and PCB-P-10; at v1.2 C901 stood 29 mm from the TPS62136 it serves and 136 of the 237 capacitors then checked were beyond these bounds. The placer places each IC's or converter's capacitors right after it (input, output, decoupling, bulk), keeps free room around it for them when the first try leaves one beyond its bound (without moving a converter more than 1 mm further from its inductor), orders parts by the area of the part and its capacitors, and then reorders, swaps and moves capacitors still beyond their bound.
