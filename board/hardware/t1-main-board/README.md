# T1 main board project

The KiCad 10 project of the T1 main board. The schematic is captured block by block ([feature-block catalog](../../docs/specs/feature-blocks.md), [RFC 0011](https://github.com/opendap-project/rfcs/blob/main/text/0011-feature-blocks-and-variants.md)); the board is at its placement stage: every part placed, nothing routed ([placement v1](../../docs/specs/placement-v1.md)). Prototype 1 targets quality level Q1 ([RFC 0010](https://github.com/opendap-project/rfcs/blob/main/text/0010-quality-levels.md)).

The schematic is generated: `generator/` writes every sheet, the project file, the library tables and the project-local library parts from the board's data. Edit the generator and run it again; never edit a generated file by hand. A rerun with no change in its inputs writes byte-identical files (every UUID is derived from a stable key).

| `t1-main-board.dru.toml`, `t1-main-board.kicad_dru` | The rule sources and the composed rules ([`../rules/`](../rules/)) |

| `../lib/symbols/T1_Main_Board.kicad_sym`, `../lib/footprints/T1_Main_Board.pretty/` | Project-only parts libs `kicad/` does not carry yet: the TPS7A1006PDSER and shield-frame symbols, the Murata DFE252012F, DFE201610E, Abracon AOTA-B201610, Coilcraft XFL3012, C&K PTS815 and Vishay WSL (doc 30100 pad table, by model and resistance range) lands, the 0.8 mm test pad (PCB-D-06) and the shield-frame lands. Generated; each land carries its libs `kicad/` envelope model (libs v0.3.3) |

## Conventions the generator applies

- Values carry their unit with an SI prefix and a space (CRAFT-141): `22 Ω`, `4.7 kΩ`, `100 nF`. Ratings the sheet's "unless noted" line does not cover go to `Spec`.
- Rails connect through power symbols named as the power tree; signals through labels, hierarchical labels where they cross the hierarchy. Global labels are not used.
- Net names are pinout v1's (active low ends in `_N`, upper case, CRAFT-84). A converter's switch node is named `<RAIL>_SW` and no other net ends in `_SW`, so the `SW_NODE` class pattern finds every switch node.
- Zone net classes (PCB-S-16, S-17): every net but GND gets the `ZONE_<NAME>` class of each zone whose blocks it touches, from the block zones of `pcb/plan.py` (`zone_patterns` in `generate.py`); a rail used in more than one zone gets `ZONE_SHARED`. Nets KiCad names from a pin (`Net-(…)`, `unconnected-(…)`) take the zone of their part's sheet.
- Output modules (feature blocks #51, #52): the 3.5 mm stage and jack (`blk.hp-out-se`) and the 4.4 mm stage and jack (`blk.hp-out-bal`) are fitted or left off whole; `generate.py` stops on a variant file that gives a module's parent and children different states. Their absent states are on the stage and jack sheets' interface tables (DAC outputs, enables, detect lines, the REFA/REFB net ties) and in the catalog record.
- Measurement links (BOM v1 line 137; numbered as power tree §4.12) carry their shunt in the base design (the full variant). The production variant fits 0 Ω jumpers (except H5), written into each link as a KiCad 10 design variant from `../variants/*.toml`; the eMMC's 16 GB alternate is written the same way. The generator writes this variant data itself; the feature-blocks spec §6 assigns it to `odapkit variant sync`, which does not exist yet.
- Parts the BOM does not name (generic capacitors and resistors) are capture choices from the Murata GRM and Yageo RC families (`generator/parts.py`). Their stock and effective capacitance under DC bias are **UNVERIFIED** until the BOM pass.
- `PWR_FLAG`s sit on the sheet that feeds each rail, on a branch of the rail's wire outside the block's groups (a link's load side, an inductor output). A local net fed through a passive part (`TPM_VDD` through its link, `RP_DVDD` through the inductor, `GAUGE_VC2` through its input resistor) stays a local net, not a power-tree rail, and takes its flag the same way: on a branch of the wire that leaves the feeding part, never in a detached label-and-flag row. KiCad 10.0.7's ERC reports a flag on a net island of power symbols alone as `pin_not_connected` (tested again on 2026-10-25, also with a project ERC exclusion), so the flags cannot go on the power overview.
- Buses cross the hierarchy through the project's bus aliases (`schematic.bus_aliases` in the `.kicad_pro`): `DDR_CHA` (with `DDR_RESET_N`), `DDR_CHB`, `EMMC`, `I2C0`, `I2C1`, `I2C2`, `PMIC_CTL`, for the audio block `AUDIO_I2S`, `AUDIO_CTL`, `DAC_L_OUT`, `DAC_R_OUT`, `HP_SE`, `HP_BAL`, and for the ports and IO `USB`, `IO_IRQ`, `IMON`, `WL`, `FP`, `EXP_IO` (the expander's port lines), `SDMMC0`, `SPK_I2S`, and for the control lines of the buttons, the I2C2 blocks and the coprocessor `KEYS` (with the cover detect, a gpio-keys switch too), `NFC`, `TPM`, `RP`, the status LED's current-source lines `LED`, and the debug UART with the spare SoC GPIOs `DEBUG`. A sheet pin `{ALIAS}` carries every member; inside a sheet the members keep local labels at their pins and one hierarchical label `{ALIAS}` on a bus stub joins them. The members keep their pinout names.
- Power symbols point up (GND down, CRAFT-85). A ground on an IC's side pin whose next row is taken leaves on a stub past its neighbours' text and drops to the first free row, where its symbol points down below the pin, never among the neighbours' labels; a ground below it on the same side joins that drop. A rail on an IC's side pin stands upright at the end of a stub long enough to clear the two rows above it; a column of rails on adjacent pins (the RK809's LDO outputs) turns its symbols outward, each value on its own row.
- Text never overlaps text or lines (CRAFT-175): fields go on a side of the body without pins, clear of capacitor plates; decoupling pitch follows the longest value or rating; table rows grow with wrapped text; a hierarchical label with a local label one row below it in the same pin column moves up half a row on a short dogleg; and `generator/overlaps.py` checks every plotted sheet (references included), group outlines and table borders among the lines. Bus ports stack above the interface table and fall back to rows when the column would meet a group. A vertical two-pin part's text keeps 1.27 mm to the next part's body.
- Zones (CRAFT-170, -174): groups, tables and the notes box never meet each other, the title block or the frame, and nothing drawn crosses the frame. The generator stops on a collision (`overlaps.zones`), and `generator/overlaps.py` reports frame and title-block crossings on the plots.
- The root (CRAFT-111) is laid out in titled rows; the page numbers are assigned from the same row list before anything is drawn, each parent's sub-sheets right after it, and the generator stops if the plot reads in another order or a block reaches the frame or the title block. Boxes stacked in one column share its width, in 7.62 mm steps. A column may leave a stack level empty, so a box sits in the next band below a shorter neighbour or the notes (checked against them); two facing sheet pins are joined directly only where the route meets no other symbol, otherwise each takes a label.
- Connector contacts on one side with one rail (VBUS, GND) join one vertical collector clear of the signal labels, one symbol at its end (`draw.side_collector`).
- Thermistors use libs' `opendap-generic:Thermistor_NTC` (reference prefix RT, CRAFT-130); KiCad's stock symbol carries TH.
- A unit of a multi-unit part reads reference, dot, unit name (`U3501.System`, `U1101.BUCK`): the project's unit notation is KiCad's `.A` (`schematic.subpart_id_separator` 46); without it KiCad 10 runs the name into the designator (`U3501System`).
- The title block's Variant shows `full` on the base design: the project text variable `OPENDAP_BASE_VARIANT` comes from the variant file of kind `full` in `../variants/`, and libs' `kicad/` drawing sheet shows it when KiCad's `${VARIANT}` is empty (a plot with `--variant <name>` shows that name).
- Group outlines are sized from their contents with a 2.54 mm margin (CRAFT-174); the shield-can frames share one group, as a group per one-symbol frame would take its width from its title.
- Clearances (`generator/overlaps.py`, on the SVG plots): no text, pin numbers included, within 0.1 mm of a no-connect cross; labels, references and values at least 0.5 mm from a component outline, pin or cross; group outlines at least 3.81 mm inside the frame.

## Commands

From the repository root, with tools' `hardware/` `odapkit` on the path:

```sh
python3 hardware/t1-main-board/generator/generate.py      # after any input changes
kicad-cli sch erc hardware/t1-main-board/t1-main-board.kicad_sch
odapkit craft check hardware/t1-main-board/t1-main-board.kicad_sch
python3 hardware/t1-main-board/generator/overlaps.py                   # text collisions on the plots
kicad-cli sch export pdf --theme OpenDAP -o fab/t1-main-board.pdf hardware/t1-main-board/t1-main-board.kicad_sch
python3 hardware/t1-main-board/pcb/build.py --drc         # the board's placement, after the schematic or pcb/ changes
odapkit dru compose hardware/t1-main-board/t1-main-board.dru.toml   # after the rule sources change
odapkit dru check
```

The generator's inputs are the pinout (`../pinout/pinout-v1.csv`), the net classes (`../rules/netclasses.toml`; set `T1_NETCLASSES` to read another copy), the variant files (`../variants/*.toml`), libs `kicad/` (`../lib/opendap`) and the block definitions in `generator/`. Rerun it when any of them changes: the net classes and their patterns in the project, the variant data and the interface lists follow.

The PDF plot uses libs' `kicad/` OpenDAP colour theme (CRAFT-103): copy `../lib/opendap/kicad/colour-themes/opendap.json` into KiCad's `colors` folder first (on macOS `~/Library/Preferences/kicad/10.0/colors/`). Plots are generated outputs and are not committed.

## Capture decisions

Details the board documents left open, decided at capture. Each sheet's notes repeat them.

- `blk.current-monitors`: Accepted (2026-10-26). The shunts sit on this sheet with their INA236, and each measured branch is a rail of its own, named in power tree §4.12: `VSYS_SRC` (BQ25798 SYS, the supply side of the VSYS shunt), `VSYS_LV_AUDIO`, `VSYS_AUDIO` (the LMR33630C with its enable shifter), `VSYS_LV_BL`, `VSYS_P5V1`; their PWR_FLAGs hang from the shunts' load-side wires. Shunt parts in the Vishay WSL family of line 43: WSL2512R0100FEA (VSYS, 10 mΩ, 1 W), WSL2010R0200FEA (HAT, 20 mΩ, 0.5 W), WSL1206R1000FEA (display), WSL1206R0200FEA (both audio channels, named by the BOM). A0 = GND, VS, SDA, SCL for 0x40-0x43; ALERT open; no input filter. Amended (Accepted, 2026-10-28, BOM v1.22 items 1-2): the display channel is an INA236B at 0x49 (A0 = VS), so no A0 = SDA strap remains, and every WSL part sits on Vishay's land for its model and range.
