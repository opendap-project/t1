# T1 product requirements

Product requirements (PRD) for the OpenDAP T1. Component requirements live in each component's repository.

| ID | Requirement | Priority | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1-001 | The T1 shall be a Digital Audio Player that is fully Open Source and Open Hardware, and runs 100% on Linux. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-002 | The T1 shall also be a general-purpose Linux gadget, extensible with HATs mounted on its back. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-003 | The T1 shall accept interchangeable faceplates. The default is the touch faceplate. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-004 | The T1 shall come in a Commercial and a DIY edition built from exactly the same design, differing only in manufacturing precision. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-005 | The Commercial edition shall be assembled. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-006 | The DIY edition shall be a kit the user builds. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-007 | The complete product shall be assembled and simulated from the exports of its components, each acting as a single unit. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-008 | Audio quality is a top priority: the T1 shall maximize sound quality and offer the features with real impact for audiophiles. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-009 | Battery autonomy is a top priority: power management shall maximize battery life. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-010 | Hardware shall prefer commercial, highly available, easy-to-source parts. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-011 | Software shall prefer the most standard Linux, open-source, trick-less option. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-012 | The battery shall be a standard commercial cell, not custom, with at least 2,000 mAh. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-013 | The device footprint shall be approximately that of a credit card (ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1: 85.60 × 53.98 mm). | must | Withdrawn |
| T1-014 | The left edge shall carry the **Action**, **Volume +** and **Volume −** buttons. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-015 | The right edge shall carry the **Main / Power** button. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-016 | The bottom edge shall carry, from left to right: a 3.5 mm jack, a 4.4 mm jack, a USB-C port (centered) and the integrated mono speaker (right side). | must | Confirmed |
| T1-017 | The Commercial edition should reach at least IP65, or the ingress protection standard of its product category. | should | Draft |
| T1-018 | The faceplate interface (`docs/specs/faceplate-interface.md`) shall define: flat-cable connector pinout, dimensions and anchoring with screws. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-019 | Faceplates are not meant to be swapped regularly. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-020 | Faceplate identification should rely on the faceplate interface pinout; an EEPROM only if the pinout cannot do it. | should | Confirmed |
| T1-021 | Coordinate system for every mechanical part: origin at the device center, x across the width, y along the length, z through the thickness with +z towards the faceplate. General tolerances ISO 2768-m. | must | Draft |
| T1-022 | The Action button shall have a deeper travel and a clearer click; the other buttons need no special click or travel. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-023 | Faceplate controls shall not send raw signals: the faceplate interface shall standardize binary commands that a DAP needs. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-024 | The command set shall have a well-standardized core that is not minimal, plus reserved space for new commands. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-025 | The T1 shall have an ADB-style command-line tool for advanced users (specified in `toolkit`). | must | Confirmed |
| T1-026 | Every technical promise shall be verified at least in software (or simulation) until it can be measured on hardware, and the tooling to debug it shall exist. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-027 | Hardware parts shall be the most recent generation available (for example the latest Wi-Fi and Bluetooth versions) as long as they stay compatible with the project: mainline Linux support, interfaces the SoC provides, and commercial availability. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-028 | Among compatible parts, prefer the ones with the best availability; when a part is not available, look for the best commercially available alternative. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-029 | Every hardware part shall be validated against real availability before it is adopted, and replaced by a part with availability when it has none. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-030 | Displays shall fit the product's dimensions; the product is never resized to fit a display. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-031 | Maximum device dimensions, excluding the side buttons and add-on HATs: 62.5 mm wide × 95 mm tall × 25 mm thick. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-032 | Ideal device dimensions, for the main body only (buttons may protrude): a credit-card footprint, rounded to 54 mm wide (ISO/IEC 7810 ID-1 is 85.60 × 53.98 mm), and up to 20 mm thick. Designs aim for the ideal and may grow up to the maximum (T1-031) only when needed. | should | Confirmed |
| T1-033 | The device should keep the proportions of a credit card (height-to-width ≈1.59, as 85.6 × 54 mm), also when it grows toward the maximum. The proportion is the ideal: adjusting it slightly is valid when it genuinely helps. | should | Confirmed |
| T1-034 | Displays shall keep an aspect ratio in about the same range as the body (height-to-width ≈1.5 to 1.6 in portrait), with **2:3 preferred**. | must | Moved |
| T1-035 | Battery capacity shall be as large as fits the defined dimensions. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-036 | The faceplate interface shall carry the signals for almost any display and touch connection standard, so that faceplates can change display or touch parts without changing the board. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-037 | Once the battery is chosen and autonomy re-estimated, the body shall shrink toward the ideal as long as audiophile-session autonomy stays around the average for this category of device (slightly below is acceptable), keeping the credit-card proportions; otherwise it stays at the size that meets autonomy. | should | Confirmed |
| T1-038 | Catalog parts come first. A custom or made-to-order part is allowed only when a small independent project can obtain it: minimum orders, tooling and process within its reach, its drawing and specification documented and publishable, and a second maker able to build it where possible. It is rejected when it needs an industrial barrier the project cannot overcome, such as minimum orders or tooling only volume brands can meet, a factory relationship, a closed process or an NDA. The display cover glass and the battery pack, a 2S pack made of catalog cells ([board ADR 0014](../../../board/docs/adr/0014-two-cell-battery.md)), are examples that meet it. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-039 | Audiophile-session autonomy thresholds: **6 h is the red line** (minimum admissible); below 8 h is a warning; 8 h or more is fine; more than 12 h is ideal. They also bound how far the body may shrink (T1-037). | must | Confirmed |
| T1-040 | The audiophile experience is the one thing that is never sacrificed: it is required at 100 %. This is an OpenDAP. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-041 | Everything else is balanced by cost and benefit, with concessions where they give the best possible product and make the most of the hardware. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-042 | No decision shall depend on obtaining a development kit (for example a Radxa ZERO 3); kits only refine estimates when available. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-043 | The T1 shall define a standard set of button combinations for system functions (power, power menu, restart, boot modes, screenshots and others), inspired by Android's conventions; for example, Volume − + Main held opens the power/restart menu and Main held powers on. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-044 | The T1 shall define a status LED code with at least a base set of states, based on existing standards where possible. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-045 | The T1 shall always use 100 % of its hardware: software never locks, caps or hides a capability the hardware has. Limits exist only for safety or because spectrum law requires them (radio power per country, DFS, indoor-only bands), and they are documented and visible to the user. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-046 | Scale shall never degrade the experience: no fixed limit on library size or indexing, and no glitches, stalls or lost data with large libraries, long playlists, large files or many installed capabilities. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-047 | Long sessions shall never degrade the experience: the T1 shall stay as fast and stable after days of use without a restart as after a fresh boot, with no growing memory use, slowdowns or accumulated glitches. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-048 | Every experience metric (start-up, responsiveness, library scale, audio output, power, endurance, radio) shall have three fixed thresholds: an ideal value, a warning value and a hard limit. Targets always aim at what the hardware allows; a release that crosses a hard limit does not ship. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-049 | The T1 shall produce no audible click or pop at power on or off, play, pause, seek, track change, sample-rate or PCM/DSD change, output change or gain change. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-050 | Volume shall be set inside the DAC in fine steps, with identical left and right attenuation, a level remembered per output, and a safe level after a power cycle. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-051 | The T1 shall resume exactly where the user left off (queue, shuffle order, repeat mode, track and position) after a pause, a power-off, a crash or a battery cut-off. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-052 | The first library import shall work without extra software or manual steps: music copied to the device or a microSD card is found, indexed in the background and playable while indexing continues. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-053 | Unplugging headphones shall pause playback, and music shall never move to the speaker unless the user chooses it. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-054 | As a USB DAC for a computer or phone, the T1 shall deliver the same audio quality as in standalone playback, bit-perfect and without added noise or delay. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-055 | The battery shall be replaceable by the user with common tools and a standard part. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-056 | The interface shall always be very fluid: every animation and scroll at the display's full frame rate and every touch or button answered within the experience budgets, at all times, including during library scans, downloads, updates, high-rate or DSD playback and low battery. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-057 | The device shall react as fluidly as its interface: the audible and physical effect of every action (volume, play, pause, seek, track change, output or gain change) follows the input within the experience budgets, and what the interface shows always matches what the device is doing. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-058 | The Commercial edition shall include the HAT device-side piece, fitted. In the DIY edition it is an optional accessory. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-059 | The physical controls follow the concept art: on the left edge, the Action button is a round disc at the top, subtly concave, and the only control in the orange brand accent; Volume + and Volume − below it are two half-capsules: together they form one capsule split across the middle, each half with a rounded outer end and a flat inner edge at the split, operated as two separate buttons. On the right edge, Main (power) is a taller capsule with horizontal ribs for touch identification. Volume and Main are silver-finished plastic; Action is the only orange control. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-060 | The body buttons shall work with the screen off, with the same response as with the screen on. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-061 | A short press of Action shall perform the current screen's primary action (with the screen off: play / pause of the active media); a long press shall perform a direct action the user chooses. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-062 | A press of Volume + / − shall change the volume by one step while sound plays, the active activity asks for the volume or the screen is off, and otherwise move the focus (Volume − to the next element, Volume + to the previous); held, it shall keep doing so; while they are the volume, a double press shall go to the next / previous track, taking back the first press's step, with the screen on or off. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-063 | No press of Action or the volume buttons shall wait for a later press: a single press acts at once. Action shall have no double or triple press; the volume buttons' only one is the track (T1-062). | must | Confirmed |
| T1-064 | The T1 shall offer audio profiles, from a monitor profile for neutral, reference listening to personal audiophile sessions with a custom profile that shapes the sound's colour and texture. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-065 | Connected by USB to a computer, the T1 shall appear by default as a standard USB storage device with an organised directory tree, so music is copied as ordinary files from any host with no extra software; OpenDAP Desktop and the `opendap` CLI are the recommended way to manage it. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-066 | The T1 shall be fully useful without OpenDAP Desktop: music bought as files (for example FLAC) and loaded onto it by any means plays like the rest of the library. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-067 | The T1 should keep a wish list, synced with OpenDAP Desktop and with the providers that support it. A provider may also offer purchase from the device in one action, only when the listener enables it in that provider's settings. | should | Draft |
| T1-068 | OpenDAP shall have a phone app. OpenDAP Desktop comes first. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-069 | If the T1 offers Bluetooth item tracking (BRD-030), it shall implement the cross-platform unwanted-tracking protections (Detecting Unwanted Location Trackers) from the first release: rotating identifiers, detection by phones of both major platforms, sound on demand, and no stable identifier in clear. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-070 | OpenDAP shall publish a dated support commitment for the T1 and an end-of-life procedure, whose last step unlocks rather than disables anything. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-071 | The T1 shall meet every mandatory regime of each market it is declared for, in the configurations listed in its technical file (faceplates, representative HATs, charging, USB device mode). Level Q3. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-072 | Both analog headphone outputs shall implement sound-dose management per EN 50332-3 through IEC 62368-1 clause 10.6: power-on level at or below RS1; a warning when the momentary level exceeds RS2 and at every 100 % of the seven-day dose; the listener's acknowledgement gives the full hardware output; without it the level lowers gradually to RS1; dose, thresholds and every reduction are visible (SYS-012). Level Q2. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-073 | Dose metering shall be passive: it never changes samples and acts only on the DAC volume, so bit-perfect playback (OS-020) and USB DAC mode (T1-054) are unchanged; the dose also runs in USB DAC mode. Level Q1. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-074 | The maximum wide-band output voltage of each headphone output, at each gain and into 16 Ω, 32 Ω, 300 Ω and open circuit, shall be measured per EN 50332-2 and published. Level Q2. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-075 | The T1 shall apply the radio regulatory domain of the country it is in, with the certified transmit power per band; access-point mode is offered only on channels the radio is certified for without radar detection. Level Q2. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-076 | The handheld T1 shall pass RF exposure evaluation as a portable device (FCC 2.1093, EN 50566, RSS-102), body-worn and extremity, with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth transmitting together and the representative HAT. Level Q3. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-077 | A certified variant shall contain no transmitter without a grant for its declared markets, and 802.15.4 stays disabled while it is not wired. Level Q3. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-078 | The T1 shall meet EN 18031-1 and EN 18031-2, and EN 18031-3 before any payment function is enabled (T1-067). Level Q3. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-079 | Every battery pack shipped, in any edition, shall hold a UN 38.3 test summary (Q2) and an IEC 62133-2 report (Q3); the pack specification (cells, PCM, connector, label) shall be published so any maker can build a replacement. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-080 | The pack and the product shall carry the labels of Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 Article 13 and the WEEE mark; replacing the battery shall need commercially available tools only (T1-055). Level Q3. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-081 | The T1 shall carry the charging-power label and the charger pictogram of Directive (EU) 2022/2380, charge at full speed from any compliant USB PD charger and at reduced speed from any 5 V USB Type-C source, with or without the OS running. Level Q3. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-082 | Every build that leaves the lab shall use RoHS-compliant parts and lead-free assembly (Q2); the technical file holds a RoHS, REACH and Proposition 65 declaration per BOM line (Q3). | must | Confirmed |
| T1-083 | Accessible surfaces shall stay within the IEC 62368-1 TS1 touch-temperature limits in the worst case: 15 W charging, full-scale headphone output, a HAT at its full budget and Wi-Fi transmitting. Level Q2. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-084 | A regulatory-information screen should show the certification identifiers and marks the authorities accept as an electronic label. Level Q3. | should | Confirmed |
| T1-085 | The HAT standard shall state host and accessory compliance responsibilities: limited-power host outputs under USB PD, ESD at the HAT port contacts, the emission budget of the HAT link, the antenna keep-out, and that each HAT carries its own declaration. Level Q3. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-086 | No safety function shall depend on software alone (battery charging, acoustic output at power-on); the hazard analysis shows it. Level Q2. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-087 | With the screen off, a double tap on a touch screen shall turn the screen on; no other touch shall act while it is off. Unlocking, where a lock is set, comes after it. | should | Confirmed |
| T1-088 | The battery pack shall connect to the T1 through contacts, with no cable, cable connector or solder joint between pack and device, so the listener replaces it by opening the battery cover with commercially available tools, lifting the pack out and placing a new one; a pack built to the published specification (T1-079) shall fit and work without any other part. Level Q1. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-089 | The T1's Wi-Fi and Bluetooth shall beat the typical commercial Digital Audio Player in Bluetooth audio stability and range (on the body, in line of sight and through walls), Wi-Fi throughput at range and radio latency, measured with OpenDAP's radio performance method against a dated benchmark set, and shall get there through engineering (antennas and their placement, receiver path, desense, coexistence, link adaptation and codecs) within each market's certified transmit power (T1-045, T1-075). Thresholds: experience budgets, "Radio". Level Q2 (hard limits), Q3 (warnings), Q4 (ideal). Level Q3. | must | Confirmed |
| T1-090 | The display of each faceplate shall use the best display technology available that meets the established product requirements (dimensions, T1-030 to T1-033; fluidity, T1-056; power and autonomy, T1-039) without a material change to the product cost. | must | Confirmed |

## Context

- T1-077 reworded on 2026-10-27 (maintainer decision; reason: board area): the NFC reader (PN7160) is removed from the main board in every model, so the clause that kept it unfitted until it had a grant is withdrawn; the NFC dynamic tag stays ([board feature blocks](../../../board/docs/specs/feature-blocks.md) #41, #42). Was: "…; the PN7160 reader stays unfitted until it has one, and 802.15.4 stays disabled while it is not wired."
- Body size (maintainers, 2026-10-27): the T1 body grows to the T1-031 maximum, 62.5 × 95.0 × 25.0 mm ([ADR 0006](../adr/0006-body-at-the-maximum-width.md), superseding ADR 0003), because the main board with its real parts needed 111 % of its area in the 59.5 × 94.3 mm body and only removing every should block would have fitted it. T1-031, T1-032 and T1-033 stay as written: T1-031 is met with no margin; **T1-032's card-size ideal is not met** (62.5 mm wide against about 54 mm) and **T1-033's proportion is 1.52** against the card's 1.59. The growth is reversed when area is freed (routing room, later parts, a thinner stack).
- T1-038 amended on 2026-10-25 (maintainers): the strict "no custom or made-to-order components" rule, whose only exceptions were the cover glass and the 2S pack, becomes a viability test. A made-to-order part is accepted when a small independent project can obtain, document and learn it, and rejected when it needs an industrial barrier. Decisions that cited the old wording keep their other reasons; they are re-checked when their component is next revised.
- T1-090 (maintainers, 2026-10-25): the display offers the best technology possible, as long as it does not alter the budget materially and changes no established requirement, the maximum dimensions included. The touch faceplate's display was re-checked against it on 2026-10-25: [faceplate-touch ADR 0001](../../../faceplates/touch/docs/adr/0001-focus-lcds-e35gh-display.md) stands (see that repository's requirements).
- T1-085 reworded on 2026-10-25 for HAT interface v2 (HATs are USB only, power under USB PD): its compliance points no longer name a connector ([RFC 0014](https://github.com/opendap-project/rfcs/blob/main/text/0014-hat-interface-v2-usb-only.md), [hat-standard ADR 0006](https://github.com/opendap-project/hats/blob/main/standard/docs/adr/0006-hats-are-usb-only.md)). T1-071 and T1-076 stand unchanged.
- T1-089 and T1-048's radio metric (maintainers' aim, 2026-10-24): targets in the [experience budgets](../specs/experience-budgets.md) Radio section, Draft until calibrated on the prototype. Level Q3: prototypes measure against it, products ship only when they meet it. T1-088 confirmed the same day: a catalog contact part meets every margin ([board ADR 0020](../../../board/docs/adr/0020-battery-connected-through-contacts.md)).
- T1-088 (2026-10-24): the maintainers' aim of a battery connected through contacts, not a cable connector, so listeners replace it easily, for the right to repair and because battery failures are a common end of life for portable players. Priority **must**: a catalog 6 A contact fits the existing board-to-pack gap with every BRD-049 margin and no loss of pack volume, and easy replacement is what T1-055 and Art. 11 of Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 promise. Design: [board ADR 0020](../../../board/docs/adr/0020-battery-connected-through-contacts.md).
- T1-045 amended and T1-071 to T1-086 added on 2026-10-24. The level is the RFC 0010 quality level at which each becomes mandatory: prototypes (Q1) are not held to Q2–Q3 items. T1-072 resolves the conflict between the headphone output of board ADR 0012 and the acoustic limits: the dose-based route of EN 50332-3 keeps the full hardware output after the listener's acknowledgement, as T1-045 requires; whether every declared market accepts this route without a fixed ceiling is **UNVERIFIED** and is the first question for the test lab.
- T1-065 (layout and handover: blackbird-os ADR 0013), T1-069, T1-070 (2026-10-23): confirmed, resolving the pending points with the project's design philosophy.
- T1-065 to T1-068 (maintainers, 2026-10-23), answering open questions on the experience across devices. T1-065: plain files, no host-specific tooling; the proposed layout (exFAT volume `OPENDAP`, `Music/`, `Podcasts/`, `Audiobooks/`, `Recordings/`, `Playlists/`, files found anywhere) is in the [user-facing conventions spec](https://github.com/opendap-project/blackbird/blob/main/system/docs/specs/user-facing-conventions.md) §1, Draft until confirmed. While the host holds the storage, the device cannot write the same filesystem (USB mass storage gives the host exclusive block access): how playback and indexing behave during that time is an engineering point for `blackbird-os`. T1-067: on-device purchase is opt-in per provider, set by the listener in that provider. T1-068: the phone app follows the desktop; its scope is not defined yet.
- T1-060 to T1-063 (maintainers, 2026-10-23): Action follows the screen's primary action, as the orange control on screen does; long-press volume for tracks follows an Android convention, applied with the screen on too so one press means one thing. Multi-press would delay the single press by Android's 300 ms double-tap window, against the experience budgets (T1-057). T1-062 amended the same day: the volume buttons move the focus when nothing plays and nothing asks for the volume (maintainers, 2026-10-23); how that meets the track long press is in the [controls spec](../specs/controls-and-status-led.md) §1.3 (one meaning per context, 2026-10-24).
- T1-062, T1-063 amended (maintainers, 2026-10-26): a held volume button keeps its press's meaning everywhere, the volume as the focus, so held means one thing in every context; the track moves to a double press that never delays the single one: the first press steps at once, and a second within 300 ms (Android's double-tap timeout) takes that step back and changes the track. Two quick presses meant to raise the volume two steps change the track instead; holding is the way to change it fast.
- T1-064 (maintainers, 2026-10-23): audio profiles are a core part of the audiophile experience. Monitor (bit-perfect), Calibrated and Personal profiles, their blocks and rules: [audio profiles spec](https://github.com/opendap-project/blackbird/blob/main/system/docs/specs/audio-profiles.md).
- T1-059 (maintainers, 2026-10-23): Volume + / − and Main changed from graphite to silver-finished plastic (metallic paint under a satin clear coat); the body stays graphite and Action orange.
- T1-059 (maintainers, 2026-10-22): the button design in the concept art (`docs/concept-art/`) governs; the design system draft describes it. Other details in that concept art are stale (480 × 800 screen, 1.27 mm pins, earlier dimensions).
- T1-049 to T1-055 (2026-10-22): moments of truth that had no must requirement.
- T1-048 (maintainers, 2026-10-22): thresholds in [`../specs/experience-budgets.md`](../specs/experience-budgets.md). Values are Draft until confirmed and calibrated on the first prototype.
- T1-046 and T1-047 (maintainers, 2026-10-22): many DAPs cap library indexing, glitch with too much data and slow down after many hours without a restart. These are product rules, verified by scale and endurance tests on every release (TK-016, TK-017).
- T1-043, T1-044: specified in [`../specs/controls-and-status-led.md`](../specs/controls-and-status-led.md) (Draft).
- **OpenDAP** is the brand; **T1** is the model and the product.
- Official faceplates: touch (default), cassette, classic. Official HATs: powerbank, NAS, camera, recorder, oscilloscope, VNA, radio.
- T1-013 (was CASE-001), T1-014 (was CASE-002), T1-015 (was CASE-003), T1-016 (was CASE-004), T1-017 (was CASE-007), T1-018 (was CASE-009), T1-019 (was CASE-010), T1-020 (was CASE-011), T1-021 (was CASE-012), T1-022 (was CASE-023), T1-023 (was CASE-026), T1-024 (was CASE-027): moved from the `case` repository on 2026-10-21, because they are product requirements, not physical-case ones.
- T1-013 (about a credit-card footprint) was replaced on 2026-10-22 by T1-031 (maximum) and T1-032 (ideal): finding a suitable display inside a credit-card footprint proved too hard.
- T1-031 and T1-032 are outer dimensions of the finished device: they already include the case walls, the display's inner frame, bezels and every other layer. Display modules and internals must fit inside them.
- T1-034 moved to `faceplate-touch` as FPT-020 on 2026-10-22: the aspect-ratio rule applies to the touch faceplate; other faceplates have their own design rules.
- Body size (2026-10-22): designs tend toward the ideal minimum, and may grow up to the maximum (T1-031) whenever the requirements need it (for example battery capacity, T1-035). At the card ratio, a 95 mm maximum height gives about 59.9 mm of width.
- The closer to credit-card size, the better, keeping the card's proportions (maintainers, 2026-10-22). This replaces an earlier preference for a 1.50 ratio.
- The thinner, the better (maintainers, 2026-10-22): thickness tends toward the 20 mm ideal or less, and grows toward 25 mm only when autonomy needs it.
- T1-039 applies to the core audiophile session (screen off, local playback). Until the RK3566 draw is measured, the check uses the pessimistic estimate: the initial battery-life estimate puts the Adafruit 328 at about 12.8 h typical (≈ 13 h) but about 5.7 h pessimistic, below the red line, so the measurement gate in ADR 0003 is decisive. Board ADR 0014 moves the T1 to a 2S pack, which costs about 9–12 % of the core session at the same pack energy (est.).
- "Credit card sized" is part of the product's identity; that is why the size ranges (T1-031, T1-032, T1-033) are strict. Any growth beyond the ideal must be justified and reversed as soon as possible.
